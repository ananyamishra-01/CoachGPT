When AI guidance isn't enough

If you're dealing with any of the following, please speak with a qualified human professional before continuing with AI-generated advice:

  • Pain, injury, or a flare-up of an existing condition
  • A diagnosed medical condition (heart, joint, metabolic, etc.)
  • Pregnancy or postpartum recovery
  • Signs of disordered eating, or a history of one
  • No progress despite consistent effort over several weeks

Find a Certified Personal Trainer

Look for one of these recognised certifications — they indicate real, tested training knowledge:

  • NASM-CPT (National Academy of Sports Medicine)
  • ACE-CPT (American Council on Exercise)
  • ISSA or NSCA-CPT

Find a Registered Dietitian

For real nutrition guidance — especially with medical conditions — look for:
